SOL Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · SOL for macOS, by Alexander Jennings (Built by AJ)

SOL collects no personal data. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't share or sell anything about you.

What stays on your Mac

Your SOL settings, such as speed, star brightness and effect frequencies, are stored only on your Mac, inside SOL's own sandboxed folder. They never leave it. Deleting SOL deletes them.

Purchases

SOL Pro is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment; SOL never sees your payment details, name or email. To know whether you have Pro, SOL asks Apple's StoreKit for your purchase record, which Apple signs and keeps. This is SOL's only network activity. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

SOL doesn't access your files, contacts, location, camera, microphone or screen contents. If you turn on Open at Login, macOS starts SOL when you log in; you can turn it off in SOL's menu or in System Settings.

Children

SOL is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/AJ-Software-LTD/sol-website.
